## Why the Vent Puzzle Stops So Many Runs

Few things are more frustrating than standing in front of a sealed vent while your oxygen timer ticks down. If you searched for **Course to Venus how to open vent**, you are probably staring at a panel that refuses to respond, or a prompt that flashes and vanishes before you can act. You are not alone — this is one of the most common sticking points in the game's opening stretch.

The reason it trips people up is simple: the vent is not a single "press to open" prop. It sits at the intersection of three systems — power, interaction range, and a control panel that has to be read in a specific order. Miss any one of those and the vent stays shut no matter how many times you mash the interact key.

This guide breaks the whole process into plain steps, lists the controls you actually need, and covers the fixes that resolve the problem in almost every case. Whether you are on your first run or your fifth attempt, the approach below will get that vent open.

## Course to Venus How to Open Vent: The Core Controls

Before you touch the panel, make sure you know what each input does. A huge share of "broken vent" complaints turn out to be a keybind issue rather than a puzzle issue. The table below covers the actions that matter for this specific interaction.

| Action | Typical Keyboard Input | Typical Controller Input | Why It Matters Here |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move | WASD | Left stick | Positions you inside the vent's interaction radius |
| Look / Aim | Mouse | Right stick | Centers the reticle on the panel face |
| Interact | E or F | X or A | Triggers the prompt when you are close enough |
| Hold Interact | Press and hold same key | Press and hold same button | Many vents require a hold, not a tap |
| Open Notes / Inventory | Tab or I | D-pad or Select | Where the vent sequence is usually recorded |
| Crouch | Ctrl or C | B or Circle | Needed to reach low-mounted vents and crawlspaces |
| Sprint | Shift | L3 / Click stick | Gets you to the vent before a timer expires |

A quick sanity check: walk up to any other interactable object in the same room — a locker, a door, a terminal. If the prompt appears there but not at the vent, your keybinds are fine and the problem is positioning or power. If no prompt appears anywhere, your input settings need attention first.

## Step-by-Step Walkthrough to Open the Vent

Work through these stages in order. Most players find the vent opens somewhere between step three and step six, and the later steps only matter if you are still stuck.

| Stage | What to Check | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Power | Is the room lit and humming? | An unpowered vent panel is inert — no prompt will ever appear |
| 2. Proximity | Stand directly under or beside the vent frame | Interaction radius is smaller than it looks |
| 3. Reticle | Aim the center dot at the panel, not the grille | The prompt is tied to the panel mesh, not the vent slats |
| 4. Prompt | Look for a small on-screen label | If it appears, you are in range and the vent is interactive |
| 5. Input | Hold the interact key for a full second | Tapping often registers as a miss |
| 6. Sequence | Match the order shown in your notes | Wrong order resets the panel silently |
| 7. Confirmation | Listen for the mechanical release | Audio confirms the vent is cycling open |

Here is the same process as a plain checklist you can follow in real time:

1. **Restore power first.** Trace the cable run from the vent back to the nearest breaker or console. If the panel light is dead, nothing else you do will matter.
2. **Clear the area.** Props, crates, and debris can block the interaction volume. Move anything that overlaps the vent frame.
3. **Align your reticle.** Aim at the flat panel beside or below the grille, not at the slats themselves.
4. **Hold, don't tap.** Press and hold the interact input until the prompt ring completes. Releasing early cancels the action.
5. **Read the panel.** If a number, symbol, or color sequence appears, cross-reference your collected notes before entering anything.
6. **Enter the sequence in order.** Panels in Course to Venus typically reset on a wrong entry rather than locking you out, so a mistake costs time, not progress.
7. **Wait for the cycle.** The vent animates open over a short beat. Do not walk away — some vents close again if the room loses power.

## Troubleshooting When the Vent Still Won't Open

If you have followed every step and the vent is still sealed, the cause is almost always one of the situations below. Community reports consistently point to these five culprits.

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| No prompt at all | Vent is unpowered | Find and reset the breaker feeding that section |
| Prompt appears, input ignored | Keybind conflict with another action | Rebind interact to a free key in settings |
| Vent opens, then instantly shuts | You stepped out of range mid-animation | Stay still until the cycle finishes |
| Panel accepts input, nothing happens | Sequence entered in the wrong order | Re-read notes and re-enter from the start |
| Prompt never appears despite power | Blocked interaction volume | Move crates, debris, or your own character's position |
| Works in one save, not another | Missed a required item earlier | Backtrack for notes, keycards, or fuses |

A note on that last row: Course to Venus occasionally gates a vent behind an item you may have walked past. If the panel lights up but refuses all input, retrace your route through the previous two rooms before assuming the game is bugged.

## Player Experience Tips and Common Mistakes

Longtime players have shared a handful of habits that make vent puzzles far less painful. These come from player experience rather than any official documentation, but they hold up across runs.

- **Read every note you pick up.** Vent sequences are frequently written down before you ever reach the vent.
- **Check your notes menu before the panel.** Opening the panel first and then hunting for the code wastes the timer window.
- **Lower your mouse sensitivity for panel work.** A steadier reticle makes small interaction zones much easier to hit.
- **Listen for audio cues.** A hum, a click, or a pressure release usually tells you the state of the vent before the visuals do.
- **Do not sprint into the interaction.** Momentum can carry you past the prompt radius.
- **Save before attempting a sequence.** It costs seconds and saves a full room replay.

## FAQ

**Does the vent in Course to Venus require a code?**

Sometimes. Some vents open with a simple hold-interact, while others are tied to a panel that expects a short sequence. If your panel lights up but does nothing, assume a sequence is required and check your notes. The general rule for **Course to Venus how to open vent** problems is: power first, position second, sequence third.

**Why does the interact prompt not show up at all?**

The most common reason is a lack of power in that section. The second most common is proximity — the interaction radius is tighter than the visual size of the vent suggests. Stand directly in front of the panel, aim at the flat surface, and try again. If prompts are missing everywhere, check your keybind settings.

**Can I open the vent without finding the notes?**

In most cases, yes, but it takes trial and error. Panels generally reset on a wrong entry instead of locking you out, so you can brute-force a short sequence if you are willing to spend the time. That said, collecting notes is faster and safer, especially if a timer is running.

**The vent opens but closes immediately. What is wrong?**

You are most likely stepping out of range during the animation. Stop moving the moment you trigger the interaction and let the cycle finish. If it still closes, the room may be losing power intermittently — check for a failing breaker or a nearby hazard draining the circuit.

With power restored, your reticle on the panel, and the interact key held for the full duration, the vent should open on your next attempt. If it does not, work the troubleshooting table top to bottom — one of those five causes covers nearly every reported case.

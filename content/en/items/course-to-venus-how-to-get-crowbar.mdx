**Do I need the crowbar to finish Course to Venus?**

In most runs, no — it is an optional utility item rather than a required story key. That said, skipping it locks you out of several side rooms, stash containers, and shortcuts that make later sections considerably easier. If you enjoy exploring, it is worth the detour.

**Can I get the crowbar later if I miss it?**

Usually yes. The area it sits in tends to remain accessible, and most players can return once they have the traversal or access tool needed to reach it. The main cost is backtracking time, so grab it on your first pass if you can.

**Why is nothing happening when I try to use the crowbar?**

**Is there a faster route to the crowbar?**

Player experience suggests the fastest path is the side passage rather than the main corridor, but it depends on how far into the chapter you are. If you are already past the region, it is usually quicker to continue forward and loop back later than to reverse the whole route.

Getting the crowbar in Course to Venus is less about skill and more about paying attention to the routes the game quietly nudges you toward. Slow down, check the side rooms, confirm the pickup in your inventory, and the rest of the map opens up from there.

## How to Test the Course to Venus Jenna Zodiac Theory Yourself

You do not need a spreadsheet, but a few notes will get you further than a hundred forum posts. The goal is to separate what the story shows from what you expect to see.

- **Log before you read.** Write down three scenes involving Jenna before you look at any theory. First impressions are the least contaminated data you will ever have.
- **Track consistency, not vibes.** Ask whether the same behavior repeats. A one-off is characterization; a pattern is design.
- **Separate dialogue from framing.** Music, lighting, and camera choices shape how you read a line. Note when the framing is doing the work instead of the writing.
- **Compare against a baseline.** Pick one other character and ask whether they share the trait. If everyone has it, it is not Jenna's signature.
- **Revisit after finishing her route.** Endings retroactively recolor earlier scenes, and that is usually when the strongest clues become visible.
